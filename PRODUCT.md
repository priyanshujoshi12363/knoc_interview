# Knoc Interviews

> Walk in ready. Walk out hired.

---

## What is Knoc Interviews?

Knoc Interviews is your personal interview coach that sits with you
during practice — and during the real thing.

It listens. It understands. And when your mind goes blank, it gives you
the one hint you need to keep going.

No awkward silences. No "umm, let me think." Just confidence.

---

## Features

### 🎯 Live Hints

When you're stuck mid-answer, Knoc gives you a quick hint about the topic
so you can keep speaking naturally. Not a script. Not a full answer. Just
the nudge you need to get back on track.

### 🎤 Practice Mode

Run full mock interviews with an AI interviewer. It asks questions, listens
to your answers, and gives you feedback — like a real interview, but with
zero pressure and unlimited retries.

### 🗣️ Speak Naturally

No typing. No tapping. Just talk. Knoc listens to your voice and responds
in real time, the way a real conversation flows.

### 🧠 Topic Awareness

Knoc understands what you're being asked. Whether it's DSA, system design,
HR questions, or behavioral rounds — it knows the topic and gives hints
that actually fit the context.

### ⚡ Real-Time Response

Hints arrive in under a second. Fast enough to feel like a thought in your
own head, not a delay that breaks your flow.

### 🇮🇳 Hinglish Ready

Built for Indian interviews. Understands English, Hindi, and the Hinglish
in between — because that's how real interviews sound here.

### 🎧 Voice Feedback

Knoc speaks back to you. Hints, follow-up questions, and feedback all come
through natural voice, so your eyes stay free and your flow stays unbroken.

### 📊 Session Summary

After each practice session, get a clean summary of what you covered,
where you hesitated, and what to work on next.

### 🔒 Private by Default

Your practice sessions are yours. Nothing is shared, nothing is sold,
nothing leaves your control.
